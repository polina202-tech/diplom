/ FAQ

Everything you need to know

What time is check-in and check-out? ▼
Check-in is available from 3:00 PM, and check-out is by 11:00 AM. Early check-in and late check-out may be available upon request, subject to availability and additional charges.
Is breakfast included in the room rate? ▼
A complimentary continental breakfast is included with all room bookings. Our breakfast is served daily from 7:00 AM to 10:30 AM in the main dining area.
Do you have parking facilities? ▼
Yes, we offer complimentary underground parking for all guests. Please inform us in advance if you require parking so we can reserve a spot for you.
What is your cancellation policy? ▼
Cancellations made 48 hours or more before check-in are free of charge. Cancellations within 48 hours of check-in will incur a fee equivalent to one night's stay.
